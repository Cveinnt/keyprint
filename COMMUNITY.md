# Build something with Keyprint

Bring a small experiment, a failing example or a better visualization. Human and
AI-assisted contributions are welcome; reproducibility and reviewed results matter.

## Try, remix, share

1. Follow [the quickstart](README.md#start-here), then enter your own prompt.
2. Start from [a recipe](examples/README.md). Export a recorded viewer so visitors
   can explore the result without downloading your model.
3. Review prompts and outputs before sharing. Keep keys and journals private.
4. Open a **Show and tell** discussion with a screenshot or video, runnable recipe,
   exact model/runtime and what your experiment does and does not establish.

[Discussions](https://github.com/Cveinnt/keyprint/discussions) are for demos,
questions and research proposals. [Issues](https://github.com/Cveinnt/keyprint/issues)
are for reproducible bugs and bounded implementation work. No contribution quota
or paid service is required. A clear failing example is a useful contribution.

## Work with your coding agent

Give your agent the repository, [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md), and one issue. Ask it to
reproduce first, implement one narrow change, run focused checks and state limits.
The [machine-readable entry point](llms.txt) links the relevant docs. Review its
patch and outputs before submitting; an agent's assertion is not test evidence.

## Submission checklist

- Exact commit, runtime and model identity where applicable.
- Minimal public input and steps; expected versus observed behavior.
- Actual outputs, including failures, truncation and unchanged pairs.
- Checks that ran and checks that skipped; resource measurements if relevant.
- No fabricated benchmarks, private content, secret keys or detector-confidence labels.

Contributors retain attribution through Git history and are welcome to link their
own demo. Keyprint is MIT licensed; preserve its license and bundled notices.
