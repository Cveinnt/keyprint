03

A result with boundaries.

What was tested

Twelve fresh task groups, two new keys and pinned Qwen3-8B on MLX. A separate, frozen sample of 500 previously unused source groups tests human controls with those same keys.

No thresholds are fitted to this screen. Every failed attempt remains in the record. Missing results cannot count as negatives.

What remains open

Detection without the original prompt. Editing and paraphrase robustness. Broader models and inference frameworks. Output quality and serving cost.

These results do not identify Anthropic’s private algorithm, establish universal compatibility or authorize a public release.

Inspect provenance and statistical assumptions

The one-sided 97.5% binomial bound assumes independent, identically distributed documents. Exact shared-field grouping does not eliminate dependencies by author, topic or near-duplicate content. Model-training overlap is unknown.

The integrity audit recomputes every retained score term and checks source selection, original prompts and literal tokens. It does not rerun the underlying model kernels.
